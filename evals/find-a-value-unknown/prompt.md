There is only a health bar, no number. Find the health value's address.

I won't be able to reply until you are done, so don't stop to ask. Each time a
scan finishes, I do the next of these, in order: press Hit me (health goes
down), press Heal (health goes up), press Hit me again, then leave it alone.
